# IRONFORGE FITNESS — Website Knowledge Base

IRONFORGE FITNESS is presented as a premium strength and performance gym founded in 2014. The website focuses on elite coaching, premium equipment, structured programs, and measurable progress.

## Programs
- Strength Training — progressive overload programming and expert form coaching.
- Weight Loss — high-intensity conditioning and nutrition guidance.
- Muscle Building — hypertrophy-focused training blocks for lean muscle growth.
- Personal Training — one-on-one coaching tailored to goals, schedule, and pace.
- Functional Fitness — full-body movement training for real-world strength, mobility, and balance.

## Memberships
- Basic — $29/month. Full gym floor access, standard equipment, locker room and showers, 1 group class/week.
- Pro — $59/month. Full gym floor access, premium equipment, locker room and showers, unlimited group classes, 2 personal training sessions. Marked Most Popular on the website.
- Elite — $99/month. 24/7 full facility access, premium equipment, private locker and towel service, unlimited group classes, unlimited personal training, 1-on-1 nutrition coaching.
